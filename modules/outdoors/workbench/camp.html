Camp editor

Click a building, the fire or a place to select it; drag it over the ground to move it.
Q / E: turn the selected building (shift: finer). Delete: remove it.
Red outline: a building on a road or touching another. Mouse: orbit (left on empty ground), pan (right), zoom (wheel).
Save writes the camp into the zone file; rebuild the zone (build.py) to see it in game.
